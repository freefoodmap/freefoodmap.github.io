# Cold Email · 教会 / 社区中心

> **收件人:** 牧师 / 社区主任 / outreach coordinator
> **目标:** 让教会成为本地联络人,验证 pantry / food cupboard 信息
> **发送时段:** 周二–周四 10:00 AM–12:00 PM 收件人本地时间
> **回复率预期:** 15–30%(教会社群紧密、个人化程度高)

---

## Subject(选一个)

### A(温暖 + 短)
```
Your {{church_name}} food pantry on a global map?
```

### B(具体)
```
Free food map for {{city}} — your {{church_name}} helps more than you know
```

### C(同行 hook)
```
Like City Harvest, but global — and your {{church_name}} is already on it
```

---

## Body

```
Subject: Your {{church_name}} food pantry on a global map?

Dear {{pastor_name}} or whoever handles {{church_name}}'s food
ministry,

I'm Andy, the founder of Free Food Map (freefoodmap.github.io) —
a volunteer-run map of 60+ free food points across 31 countries.
It's small (~50KB static site, no ads, no money exchanged) but
it reaches people who, for whatever reason, can't find their
local food bank on Google.

Your {{church_name}} food pantry at {{address}} is listed as:

  {{point_name}}
  {{schedule}}
  {{phone}}

I'm writing for two reasons:

1. **To say thank you.** I learned about your pantry from
   {{source: e.g. "your own website" / "City Harvest's referral
   list" / "a {{city}} community directory"}}. Whatever you're
   doing, it's working. The map just makes it slightly easier
   for someone in {{city}} to find you.

2. **To ask a small thing.** When someone lands on your listing
   from our site, they'd ideally see:
   - Current hours (please tell me if {{current_hours}} is still
     right)
   - Any eligibility requirements
   - Whether donations are accepted
   - The name of a real human to contact

   I can do the data entry if you can do a 2-min "yes that's
   right" / "here's the corrected version" reply.

The site is built by volunteers in their spare time. It's MIT-
licensed and explicitly non-commercial. We don't sell, track,
or monetize. We don't accept donations yet (and we wouldn't
without a partner NGO handling it).

If you want to be removed from the list, one email and it's
gone. No questions asked.

If you know other food pantries in {{city}} that aren't on the
map yet, please forward their names — I'll add them.

Blessings,

Andy Zhuang
founder, Free Food Map
https://freefoodmap.github.io
open@freefoodmap.org
```

---

## 个性化占位符

- `{{pastor_name}}` — 牧师名(或 "the food ministry team")
- `{{church_name}}` — 教会名
- `{{address}}` — 教会地址
- `{{point_name}}` — 数据里的名称
- `{{schedule}}` — 发放时间
- `{{phone}}` — 联系电话
- `{{current_hours}}` — 我们记录的时间
- `{{source}}` — 信息来源(很重要!教会喜欢"如何找到的")
- `{{city}}` — 城市

## 跟进节奏(7-21 天)

### Day 7

```
Subject: re: Your {{church_name}} food pantry on a global map?

Hi again,

Just bumping this. No need for a long reply — even a thumbs up
emoji or "looks right" is great.

If you don't have time, that's totally fine. The current data
will stay on the map as-is.

— Andy
```

### Day 21 — 升级

Forward to the church's outreach coordinator 或 deacon board:

```
Subject: re: Your {{church_name}} food pantry on a global map?

Hi,

Bumping this one last time. If your team is too busy, no worries.
If there's a volunteer at {{church_name}} who handles the food
pantry website or social media, they might be a quicker reply.

The map has ~100 visitors/month and your {{church_name}} gets
~5 of them. Small number, but for those 5, it might be the
difference between finding food and not.

— Andy
```

## 关键差异(跟 NGO 邮件比)

| 维度 | NGO 邮件 | 教会/社区邮件 |
|---|---|---|
| **语气** | 正式、数据化 | 温暖、个人化、"blessings" |
| **承诺** | "backlink" "data partner" | "thank you" "let me know" |
| **数据** | 强调 metrics | 强调 "for those 5 people" |
| **结尾** | "Thanks" | "Blessings" / "Peace" / 不用 |
| **跟进** | 14–45 天 | 7–21 天(更快) |

## 找教会联系人

1. **Google Maps:** 搜 "food pantry {{city}}",看每家 church 的 website
2. **网站:** 找 "Outreach" / "Missions" / "Food Ministry" 页面
3. **Facebook:** 搜 "{{church_name}} {{city}}"
4. **Yellow Pages / 当地 directory:** 老的反而全
5. **问朋友:** 谁住在那,谁就认识

---

## 关键成功因素

- **称呼:** "Dear Pastor {{name}}" 比 "Hi" 更合适教会
- **不要"AI / 区块链 / 颠覆"** — 教会不在乎这个
- **小数字反而有效:** "your 5 monthly visitors" 比 "1k users" 更打动人
- **退出容易:** "1 邮件下架" 关键
- **别 push 太紧:** 教会 priority 跟 startup 不一样
