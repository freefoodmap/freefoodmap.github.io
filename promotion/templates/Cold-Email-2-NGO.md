# Cold Email · 食物银行 / NGO 公益组织

> **收件人:** 食物银行、NGO 的 partnerships / communications / digital 部门负责人
> **目标:** 拿到**官方数据授权**(让你直接镜像他们官网的免费食品发放点列表)
> **发送时段:** 周二/周三 9:00 AM 收件人本地时间
> **回复率预期:** 3–8%(NGO 邮件量大、回复慢)

---

## Subject(选一个)

### A(具体 + 互利)
```
Mapping your {{org_name}} locations to a global free food directory
```

### B(数据角度)
```
1 of 60+ free food points — your {{org_name}} {{city}} branch
```

### C(短,直球)
```
{{org_name}} × Free Food Map
```

---

## Body

```
Subject: {{org_name}} × Free Food Map

Hi {{first_name}},

I'm Andy, the founder of Free Food Map (freefoodmap.github.io) —
a volunteer-run, MIT-licensed map of 60+ free food distribution
points across 31 countries. The site is bilingual (zh + en),
served via GitHub Pages, and explicitly NOT commercial.

Your {{org_name}} {{city}} branch is currently listed as:

  {{point_name}}
  {{address}}
  {{phone}}
  Source: {{link}}

I'm reaching out for two things, in order of importance:

1. **Accuracy check.** Is the above still correct as of {{month}} 2026?
   If anything has changed (hours, address, eligibility, services
   offered), please reply with the corrected version. I'll update
   the site within 24 hours.

2. **Inclusion in our next milestone.** When we hit 100+ verified
   points, we'd love to send you a small write-up — like a one-pager
   on the trends we're seeing (e.g. "10 most food-insecure regions",
   "growth of smart cabinets in 2026", etc.) — and credit {{org_name}}
   as a data partner.

   You would get:
   - Backlink from freefoodmap.github.io to your {{org_name}} site
   - Inclusion in our public data source list (already includes
     Feeding America, Trussell Trust, European Food Banks Federation,
     Oasis Food Bank, KFC food cabinets, Robin Hood Army, Food Bank
     Singapore)
   - A direct line to me for any time-sensitive community-resource
     questions from our visitors

   You would NOT get:
   - Any user data (we don't collect any)
   - Any marketing exposure (the site is explicitly non-commercial)
   - Any cost (the project is funded out of pocket)

If you're not the right person, who handles partnerships or
communications at {{org_name}}? I'd appreciate a forward.

The project is small (~50KB static site, ~100 GitHub stars, ~3
contributors). It's not going to make you famous. But for the
~5% of our visitors who land on your listing each month, it
might make the difference between "I didn't know you existed"
and "I came in for a hot meal".

Thanks for your time.

— Andy Zhuang
founder, Free Food Map
https://freefoodmap.github.io
open@freefoodmap.org
P.S. The site is at zero cost to you. If you ever want it taken
down, one email and it's gone — no questions, no legalese.
```

## 个性化占位符(必填)

- `{{first_name}}` — 收件人 first name
- `{{org_name}}` — 机构名(Feeding America, Trussell Trust...)
- `{{city}}` — 该分支所在城市
- `{{point_name}}` — 该点的全名
- `{{address}}` — 该点地址
- `{{phone}}` — 电话
- `{{link}}` — 来自 `data.js` 里的 `url` 或 `source`
- `{{month}}` — 当前月份(July / August...)

## 跟进节奏(7-21-45 天)

### Day 7 — 友好 follow-up

```
Subject: re: {{org_name}} × Free Food Map

Hi {{first_name}},

Wanted to make sure this reached you. Even a 1-line "looks
correct" or "we've moved" would be huge.

If your team is too slammed, can you point me to whoever
handles partnerships?

— Andy
```

### Day 21 — 升级(higher-up)

如果 7 天没回,转发给 CEO / Executive Director / Communications Director:

```
Subject: {{org_name}} × Free Food Map (cc: your comms lead)

Hi {{ceo_first_name}},

Forgive the direct reach. I sent a note to your {{comms_lead}}
role 3 weeks ago about a small volunteer project (Free Food Map)
that lists {{org_name}} {{city}} as one of 60+ global points.

I think {{org_name}} would benefit from being explicitly listed
as a data partner — the site has 100+ monthly visitors looking
specifically for free food, and ~5% land on your listing.

If a 5-minute reply isn't feasible, could you point me to
whoever handles partnerships?

Thanks.

— Andy
```

### Day 45 — 结案

移到 `cold-leads/nurture-2026Q4.md`。6 个月后再试 — NGO 换人慢,但换人之后是新人。

## 找 NGO 联系人

1. **官网 footer:** "Contact us" → 找 partnerships / press
2. **LinkedIn:** `{{org_name}} partnerships` 搜
3. **Annual report:** 通常有 communications director 邮箱
4. **Twitter / 微博:** 直接 DM(成功率 2–5%,但成本低)
5. **Email pattern:** 试 `partnerships@`、`press@`、`info@`、`hello@`

---

## 关键成功因素

| 因素 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| **具体** | 引用他们的具体地址、电话,而不是 generic 邮件 |
| **低承诺** | "1 行回复就够",不要让他们觉得要开会 |
| **互利** | 明确列出他们得到什么(backlink、credit、用户) |
| **退出容易** | "1 邮件就下架"消除法律顾虑 |
| **不要钱** | "零成本" "非商业" 反复强调 |
| **附 P.S.** | P.S. "如果你想下架,1 邮件就好" 是 killer line |
